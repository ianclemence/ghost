# Network Nudge

Help the user maintain weak-tie relationships and keep their professional network warm.

## 1. Find Dormant VIPs
Search `curated-memory.md` or recent emails for important people (friends, mentors, past colleagues) who haven't been mentioned in over 60 days.

## 2. Find a Hook
For 1 or 2 of these people, do a quick web search (`browser_navigate`) or check if they've been mentioned in the news or on LinkedIn. Alternatively, use a generic but warm check-in reason.

## 3. Propose the Nudge

```
🤝 **Network Check-in Nudge**

It's been a while since you caught up with some key people in your network. 

**1. [Person Name]**
You haven't spoken since [Month/Event]. 
*Suggested Draft:*
"Hey [Name], it's been a minute! Just wanted to say hi and see how things are going with [their company/project]. Let me know if you are free for a quick coffee next week!"

**2. [Person Name 2]**
*Suggested Draft:*
"Hey [Name], was just thinking about the work we did on [Past Project]. Hope everything is great on your end!"

*Copy one of these and send it off! Keeping a warm network pays dividends.*
```
