# Plan Your Week

A quick Sunday evening session to review the week ahead, set top priorities, and walk into Monday feeling prepared.

## 1. Review Upcoming Calendar
Use `session_search` or your calendar tool to pull all events, meetings, and appointments for the next 7 days.
- Flag days with heavy schedules or back-to-back meetings.
- Look for double-bookings.

## 2. Set Top 3 Priorities
Determine the 3 most important things the user should accomplish this week based on their recent activity and projects. 

## 3. Monday Prep
Highlight what is happening tomorrow specifically (early meetings, deadlines).

## 4. Share the Plan
Deliver a clean summary of the week ahead:

```
📅 **Week Ahead Plan**

Let's get organized for the week!

**THE WEEK AT A GLANCE:**
- **Monday:** [Summary of Monday]
- **Tuesday:** [Summary of Tuesday]
- **Wednesday:** [Summary of Wednesday]
- **Thursday:** [Summary of Thursday]
- **Friday:** [Summary of Friday]

**🎯 TOP 3 PRIORITIES:**
1. [Priority 1]
2. [Priority 2]
3. [Priority 3]

**MONDAY PREP:**
- [Prep item 1]
- [Prep item 2]

*Have a great evening and a strong start tomorrow!*
```
